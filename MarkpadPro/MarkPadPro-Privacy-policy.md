# MarkPadPro Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-01

MarkPadPro is a Markdown viewer and editor for Windows, published in the Microsoft Store by Technocrux (Devride, https://www.devride.com). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, **we collect nothing.**

## What we collect

Nothing. MarkPadPro has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no telemetry. We do not run a server for the app, and the app never sends your documents, settings or usage data to us or to anyone else.

## What the app stores on your PC

MarkPadPro keeps a few things in its own app data folder on your PC (the app's LocalState folder) so it can remember your preferences:

- **Settings**: theme, reading style, fonts, default view and similar options.
- **Recent files**: the file paths of up to 15 documents you opened recently.
- **Open tabs**: the file paths of the tabs you had open, so they can be restored next time. Only paths are stored, not the contents of your documents.
- **Display cache**: the Windows component that draws documents (Microsoft Edge WebView2) keeps its own working files in the same folder, for example cached copies of images shown in a document.

This data never leaves your PC. You can clear the recent files list from the start screen or the File menu. Uninstalling the app, or using Reset in Windows Settings, deletes all of this data.

## Your documents

Your documents stay where you save them. MarkPadPro reads and writes only the files you open, save or export. When you paste an image into a saved document, the image is saved in an `images` folder next to that document.

## When the app goes online

MarkPadPro works offline, and every rendering library is built into the app. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **Remote images in a document.** If a document you open links to an image on the web (for example `https://example.com/picture.png`), the app downloads that image to show it, just as a web browser would. The server hosting the image can see your IP address and the request. The app does not load remote content unless the document itself refers to it.
- **Links you click.** Web links open in your default browser only when you click them. From then on, your browser's privacy policy applies.

## Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not have any. The only other party involved is **Microsoft**:

- The **Microsoft Store** handles the purchase, payment and license. Microsoft's privacy statement covers that data: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
- Windows itself may send diagnostic data or crash reports to Microsoft, depending on your Windows privacy settings. Microsoft may give app publishers anonymous crash and usage summaries through its developer dashboard. MarkPadPro does not add anything to this.

## Children

MarkPadPro does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
