## 1. Summary

MindMapX is a mind mapping app that runs on your own PC.

- Your mind maps stay on your PC. We never upload them, and we cannot read them.
- We collect the small amount of account information needed to sign you in: your name, your email address, and your password.
- The only piece of information about your use of the app that leaves your device is the date your free trial started.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. We do not sell your personal information.
- MindMapX needs an internet connection to sign in. Once you are signed in, your maps are created and saved locally.

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Account information

When you create an account we collect:

| Data | Why we collect it |
| --- | --- |
| Full name | To identify your account and address you in the app |
| Email address | To identify your account, verify it, and send account emails such as verification and password-reset codes |
| Password | To authenticate you |

Account sign-in and account management are handled by **Flyo**, a first-party backend service operated by Technocrux. Flyo is not a third-party company; it is our own service.

### 3.2 Account security information

- **Email verification.** After sign-up we email you a 6-digit code to confirm you control the email address.
- **Two-factor authentication (optional).** If you enable it, you can sign in with a code from an authenticator app, or with a recovery code. Enabling this stores the information needed to check those codes against your account.
- **Password reset.** If you ask to reset your password, we email you a 6-digit code that is valid for 10 minutes.
- **Sign-in token.** After you sign in, an authentication token is stored on your PC. It is protected using Windows DPAPI, scoped to your Windows user account. The "Remember me" option controls whether that token is kept between sessions or discarded when you close the app.

### 3.3 Trial information

MindMapX includes a 7-day free trial. The **date your trial started** is recorded on your account as well as on your device. We do this so the trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling the app or changing your PC's clock.

This trial start date is **the only information about your use of the app that is sent from your device to us.**

### 3.4 Technical information received when you connect

Like any internet service, our servers and the CDN described in section 6.2 necessarily receive your IP address and basic connection information in order to respond to a request. We do not use this to build a profile of you, and we do not combine it with your maps (which we never receive).

## 4. Information that never leaves your device

The following is created and stored **only on your PC**:

- All mind maps, including topic text, notes, tasks, markers, attached images and recorded voice notes.
- Maps are saved as `.mflow` / `.mindflow` JSON files under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MindMapX\Maps`, together with an `AutoSave` folder.

MindMapX has **no cloud sync, no sharing, no collaboration features, and no cross-device access.** We have no copy of your maps and no technical way to retrieve them.

> **Please back up your maps.** Because your maps exist only on your PC, they are your responsibility to back up. If your PC is lost, reset, or the files are deleted, we cannot restore them for you. Copy the `%LOCALAPPDATA%\MindMapX\Maps` folder to a backup location, or use your own backup software.

## 5. Device permissions

### 5.1 Microphone

MindMapX can record short voice notes of up to 30 seconds and attach them to a topic. The microphone is used only when you start such a recording. The recorded audio is embedded in the local map file on your PC. **It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.**

### 5.2 Images and files

You can attach images that you choose from your own PC. They are embedded in the local map file. They are not uploaded.

## 6. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. Information reaches other parties only in these limited ways.

### 6.1 Microsoft (payments and subscription status)

Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by **Microsoft** through the Microsoft Store. Technocrux never sees, receives or stores your payment card details. The app asks the Microsoft Store whether your account has an active subscription; it receives only that status.

Microsoft's handling of your purchase is governed by Microsoft's own privacy statement and Microsoft Store terms.

### 6.2 jsDelivr CDN (icon stickers)

Icon stickers used in the app are downloaded at the time you use them from the public **jsDelivr** CDN (`cdn.jsdelivr.net`). As with loading any image from the internet, that CDN can see your IP address and the request. No account information and no map content is sent with these requests.

This is the only third-party network call MindMapX makes, apart from our own authentication service.

### 6.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose account information if we are legally required to do so, for example in response to a valid legal order. We cannot disclose your maps, because we do not have them.

## 8. How long we keep information

- **Account information** is kept for as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete the account record and its associated authentication data.
- **Verification and password-reset codes** are short-lived; a password-reset code expires after 10 minutes.
- **The trial start date** is kept with your account for as long as the account exists.
- **Your maps** are kept on your PC until you delete them. Deleting them is entirely under your control, and deleting your account does not delete them.
