Lever Panic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Lever Panic is made by Technocrux (Devride).

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Lever Panic on Android.

What we collect

Nothing directly. Lever Panic has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your game record (choices, scores and settings) is stored only on your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or tap "Wipe my record". If your device backs up app data to your Google account, that backup is handled by Google under your device settings.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve, limit the frequency of and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings & Privacy → Manage ad privacy choices.

Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Sharing

We don't sell or share personal information. When you tap "Share my verdict", the app opens Android's share sheet with a short text message. You choose where it goes.

Children

Lever Panic is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the copy inside the app.
