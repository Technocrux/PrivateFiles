# Digigrain Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Digigrain is made by Technocrux. This policy covers the Digigrain apps for Windows and Android.

## The short version

Digigrain does not collect, send or sell any data. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads and
no analytics. Your photos never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

## What Digigrain uses on your device

- **Camera.** To take photos when you press the shutter. The camera is used only while the
  camera screen is open. Digigrain does not record audio.
- **Photos you pick.** When you import or drop a photo, Digigrain reads it to apply a look.
  It never changes the original file.
- **Storage.** Finished photos are saved to your device: on Android in the "Digigrain"
  gallery album and the app's own folder; on Windows in `Pictures\Digigrain` (or the folder
  you choose). A copy of each original is kept in the app's private folder so you can try
  another look later. Deleting a photo in Digigrain removes both.
- **Settings.** Your choices (theme, default camera look, date format) are stored on the device.

## Internet

The Android app does not request internet access. The Windows app makes no network calls of
its own; Microsoft Store checks your licence through Windows.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's share sheet opens and you choose where the photo goes.
That app's privacy policy then applies.

## Children

Digigrain collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
