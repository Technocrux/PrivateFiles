## The short version

CutePets does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no
accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry. Everything
your pets do stays on your computer.

## What data CutePets stores

CutePets keeps a single save file on your own device:

```
%APPDATA%\cutepets\cutepets.json
```

That file contains only the game state you created yourself:

- the pets you adopted — names, species, colours, ages and inherited traits;
- their needs, coins and lifetime statistics;
- memorials of pets that have passed on, and the family tree linking them;
- your settings (graphics mode, aging speed, autostart, notification choices);
- a record of which companion packs are unlocked on this installation.

There is no name, email address, location, contact list, document, photograph,
browsing history or device identifier in that file. CutePets never reads any
other file on your computer.

## What CutePets sends over the network

Nothing. CutePets makes no outbound network requests. It does not check for
updates itself, does not load remote content, and does not contact any server
operated by _[YOUR COMPANY OR NAME]_ — because no such server exists. The app's
window security policy blocks remote connections outright; the only resources it
loads are files bundled inside the app itself.

## Purchases

CutePets offers two optional one-off purchases, the Friends Pack and the Premium
Pack. **Those transactions are handled entirely by Microsoft.** CutePets asks
Windows whether your Microsoft account owns a pack and receives a yes or a no.

- We never see, receive or store your payment details, billing address, Microsoft
  account name, or any part of your Microsoft account.
- The only thing written to disk is a flag recording that a pack is unlocked.
- Microsoft's own privacy statement governs the purchase itself:
  <https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement>

## Notifications

CutePets can raise standard Windows desktop notifications (for example, when a
pet is hungry). These are produced locally by Windows and are not transmitted
anywhere. You can turn them off in **Settings → Notifications** inside the app,
or in Windows Settings.

## Children

CutePets is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data at all, it
collects nothing from children either. It contains no advertising, no external
links inside gameplay, no chat, and no user-to-user communication.

## Your control over your data

- **See it:** open `%APPDATA%\cutepets\cutepets.json` in any text editor.
- **Back it up:** copy that file.
- **Erase it:** use **Settings → Start over** inside the app, or delete the
  `%APPDATA%\cutepets` folder. Uninstalling CutePets deliberately leaves the save
  behind so a reinstall does not lose your pets; delete the folder yourself if
  you want it gone.

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or
delete on your behalf under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA or any comparable
law. Your purchase record is held by Microsoft under their privacy statement.

## Third-party components

CutePets bundles open-source software and CC0-licensed 3D models. None of them
phone home; they are compiled into the app and run locally. Attributions are
listed in `ATTRIBUTION.md`, shipped with the app.
